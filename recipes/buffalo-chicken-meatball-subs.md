# Buffalo Chicken Meatball Subs

Blue cheese and scallion turkey meatballs, tossed in buffalo sauce and piled into a hoagie roll with ranch and crunchy toppings.

## Ingredients

**Meatballs**
- 3 lbs ground turkey
- ¾ cup breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 oz crumbled blue cheese
- ¼ cup buffalo sauce, plus more for tossing
- 1 bunch scallions
- 3 eggs
- 3 garlic cloves

**Subs**
- Amoroso hoagie rolls (1 per sub)
- Shredded lettuce
- Sliced tomato
- Pickles
- Ranch dressing

## Equipment

- Large mixing bowl
- Blender
- Ice cream scoop
- Baking sheet + silicone mat (or parchment paper)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 550°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the ground turkey, blue cheese, salt, and breadcrumbs.
3. In a blender, blend the eggs, ¼ cup buffalo sauce, scallions, and garlic.
4. Pour the wet mixture into the meat and mix until combined.
5. Use an ice cream scoop to portion meatballs onto a lined baking sheet.
6. Bake at 550°F for 12 minutes.
7. Douse the meatballs in more buffalo sauce and toss to coat.
8. Slice a hoagie roll in half, spread with ranch, and add lettuce, tomato, pickles, and meatballs.
