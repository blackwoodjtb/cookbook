# Caprese Chicken Salad Meal Prep Bowls

Balsamic baked chicken over quinoa with cherry tomatoes, mini mozzarella, and fresh basil. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

## Ingredients

**Bowls**
- ¾ cup quinoa, uncooked
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 2 large)
- Salt and pepper
- 3 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 bunch basil, leaves picked off the stems
- 1 cup baby bocconcini (mini fresh mozzarella balls)

**Balsamic Vinaigrette**
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper

## Equipment

- Saucepan (for quinoa)
- Baking dish
- Cutting board and knife
- Dressing shaker or mason jar
- 4 meal prep containers (2 cups or larger)
- Small condiment containers

## Instructions

1. Cook the quinoa according to the package and let it cool.
2. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
3. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp balsamic, place in a baking dish, and season with salt and pepper.
4. Bake about 25 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F.
5. Rest the chicken at least 10 minutes (ideally until cool), then slice against the grain.
6. Shake the vinaigrette ingredients together in a jar and portion into small containers.
7. Divide the quinoa, chicken, tomatoes, and bocconcini among 4 containers. Keep the basil separate if you can.
8. To serve, tear the basil over the bowl, drizzle with dressing, and toss.

## Notes

- Fridge up to 4 days. Don't freeze.
- Add dressing only when eating, and keep basil dry (wrap in a paper towel) so it stays fresh.
- Tomatoes get soft and juicy by day 3–4, which works fine here.
- Swaps: chicken thighs, chickpeas, or tofu for the chicken; farro, rice, or barley for the quinoa; a tortilla or pita for a wrap.
- Low-carb: cauliflower rice instead of quinoa and a sugar-free sweetener instead of maple syrup.
