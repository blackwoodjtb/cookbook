# Classic Shepherd's Pie

Herby ground beef (or lamb) and veggies in a rich gravy, topped with buttery parmesan mashed potatoes and baked until golden.

## Ingredients

**Meat Filling**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup yellow onion, chopped
- 1 lb ground beef (90% lean), or ground lamb
- 2 tsp dried parsley
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
- ½ cup frozen corn

**Potato Topping**
- 1 ½–2 lb russet potatoes (about 2 large), peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 8 tbsp unsalted butter (1 stick)
- ⅓ cup half-and-half
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ¼ cup grated parmesan cheese

## Equipment

- Large skillet
- Wooden spoon
- Large pot
- Colander
- Potato masher
- 9x9-inch (or 7x11-inch) baking dish
- Rimmed baking sheet (optional, to catch drips)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 2 minutes. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
2. Add the beef, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Add the parsley, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper and cook 6–8 minutes, until browned.
3. Stir in the Worcestershire sauce and garlic and cook 1 minute.
4. Stir in the flour and tomato paste until no clumps of tomato paste remain.
5. Add the broth, peas and carrots, and corn. Bring to a boil, then simmer 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Set aside.
6. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
7. Cover the potatoes with water in a large pot, bring to a boil, then simmer 10–15 minutes until fork-tender.
8. Drain, return the potatoes to the hot pot, and let sit 1 minute so the extra water steams off.
9. Add the butter, half-and-half, garlic powder, salt, and pepper and mash until smooth. Stir in the parmesan.
10. Spread the meat mixture in the baking dish, then spread the mashed potatoes evenly on top.
11. If the dish is very full, set it on a rimmed baking sheet. Bake uncovered at 400°F for 25–30 minutes.
12. Let cool 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Shepherd's pie is traditionally made with lamb; with beef it's technically "cottage pie." Both work.
- Make ahead: prepare the filling, the potatoes, or the whole assembled dish, cover, and refrigerate. Bake within 3 days. Take it out of the fridge about 20 minutes before baking.
- Servings are an estimate (the site doesn't list them); a 9x9 dish feeds about 6.
- Good with roasted broccoli on the side.
