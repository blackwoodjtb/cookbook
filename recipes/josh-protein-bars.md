# Josh's Protein Bars

A baked egg, sausage, and veggie slab cut into bars. Good for meal prep: make once, wrap, and grab all week.

## Ingredients

- 1 ½ lbs sausage
- 15 eggs
- 10 button mushrooms
- 20 cherry tomatoes, sliced
- 1 container (5 oz) baby spinach
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Equipment

- Sauté pan
- Silicone spatula
- Large mixing bowl
- Whisk
- Deep baking dish
- Parchment paper
- Cutting board
- Knife

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Heat a large sauté pan on high heat and brown the sausage.
3. Add the mushrooms and sauté until browned, about 3 minutes.
4. Add the spinach and cook 1 more minute, until slightly browned.
5. Add the sliced cherry tomatoes, stir, and take the pan off the heat.
6. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, salt, pepper, and cheese together.
7. Add the sausage and vegetables to the egg mixture and whisk to combine.
8. Line a deep baking dish with parchment paper and pour in the egg mixture.
9. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes, until set.
10. Let cool for 20 minutes, then turn out, cut into 5 portions, and wrap.

## Notes

- Cook time includes ~10 min on the stovetop plus 40 min baking. Allow 20 min extra for cooling.
- The original doesn't say to slice the mushrooms, but slicing them helps them brown.
