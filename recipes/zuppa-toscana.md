# Zuppa Toscana

A creamy potato, sausage, and kale soup in the style of the Olive Garden classic.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb spicy Italian ground sausage (use mild for kids)
- 4 tbsp butter
- ½ white onion, diced
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups water
- 4–5 yellow potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 4 cups chopped kale
- Optional toppings: chopped bacon or bacon bits, grated parmesan

## Equipment

- Large pot
- Slotted spoon
- Plate
- Cutting board
- Knife

## Instructions

1. Brown the sausage in a large pot, about 5–6 minutes.
2. Lift the sausage out with a slotted spoon onto a plate and set aside.
3. In the same pot over medium heat, melt the butter and cook the onion until translucent.
4. Add the garlic and cook about 1 minute, until fragrant.
5. Add the broth, water, potatoes, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil and cook until the potatoes are tender.
6. Stir in the kale and heavy cream, then return the sausage to the pot.
7. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
8. Serve topped with parmesan and/or bacon if you like.

Using half-and-half instead of cream saves about 200 calories and 30 g fat per bowl; it's also very salty (3 tsp salt plus broth), so start with less._

## Notes

- Using plain (non-Italian) sausage? Add 1 tsp dried Italian seasoning or Herbes de Provence and a pinch of red pepper flakes.
- Instant Pot: sauté sausage and onion, pressure cook everything except kale and cream on High for ~10 min, quick release, then stir in kale and cream and let sit a few minutes.
- Freezes for 2–3 weeks.
- Lighter version: poultry sausage and half-and-half.
